## Level 4 · HOTS

### Section A · 1 mark each

1. The sum of the first n terms of an AP is Sₙ = 2n² + 3n. What is its common difference?

5. How many terms are common to the APs 3, 7, 11, …, 407 and 2, 9, 16, …, 709?

7. If a, b, c are in AP (with a ≠ c), what is the value of ((a − c)²)/(b² − ac)?
    - (a) 1   (b) 2   (c) 4   (d) 8

8. **Assertion (A):** The sequence whose sum of first n terms is Sₙ = 3n² − n is an AP. **Reason (R):** If Sₙ is a quadratic in n with no constant term, the terms form an AP.

    - (b) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
    - (c) A is true, but R is false.
    - (d) A is false, but R is true.

9. **Assertion (A):** The sequence with nth term aₙ = n² + 1 is not an AP. **Reason (R):** For this sequence, a₂ − a₁ = 3.

    - (c) A is true, but R is false.
